# Image generation and editing

Use this skill when creating or editing bitmap visuals for the Nobelova Gradani website.

## Visual direction

- Keep visuals warm, calm, child-centred, and professional.
- For ABA consultation scenes, show collaboration between the behavior analyst, caregiver, and child.
- Prefer consent-aware, privacy-safe compositions with no readable client records or identifying details.
- Include visual cues such as a support plan, notes, visual schedule, or simple progress materials without exposing sensitive information.
- Preserve the website palette: deep purple, soft teal, warm gold, cream, and natural skin tones.

## Website requirements

- Design for responsive cards and hero sections.
- Keep the important subject matter near the visual centre so `object-fit: cover` works on desktop and mobile.
- Use descriptive, accessible alt text that explains the scene rather than making unsupported clinical claims.
- Prefer local assets in the project `img/` directory.
- Verify that the final asset loads in the browser and does not introduce horizontal overflow.

## Delivery checklist

1. Choose or create a visual that matches the section's purpose.
2. Save the asset in the project's `img/` directory.
3. Update the relevant HTML image source and alt text.
4. Reload the page and verify the image at desktop and mobile widths.
