Developmental Surveillance, Pediatric Applied Behavior Analysis Services (PABA), School Health Screening & Training

Since 2012, Nobelova Gradani has partnered with families, schools, and organisations across Nigeria to support child development, education, workforce development, and professional capacity building. Our services include developmental screening, educational assessments, evidence-based Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) therapy, school health screening, parent training, professional training, international credentialing, and recruitment assessments for human resources.

Our programmes are guided by evidence-based practices and professional standards. Our professional training and credentialing pathways are aligned with recognised international frameworks and credentialing standards, including the Qualified Applied Behavior Analysis Credentialing Board (QABA) and the International Behavior Analysis Organization (IBAO).

Nobelova Gradani is also accredited by the Skills Development Council Canada and is a member of the International Association of Colleges and Universities (IACU), reflecting our commitment to high standards in education, assessment, professional development, and international professional recognition.

Through collaboration with families, schools, educators, practitioners, and organisations, we are committed to promoting meaningful developmental and educational outcomes, empowering parents and caregivers, strengthening professional capacity, supporting informed recruitment and workforce decisions, and creating pathways for professionals to pursue internationally recognised credentials.

Our overarching commitment is to create opportunities for children, families, professionals, and organisations to learn, develop, and thrive.

Our services in detail

Service 01

Developmental Surveillance: screening, psychoeducation, and early-support plans

Most learning difficulties are developmental before they are academic. Our play-based assessments look at how a child moves, speaks, plays, attends and regulates — then translate the results into practical guidance for school and home.

  • Gross & fine motor assessment
  • Language & early literacy checks
  • Social & emotional screening
  • Parent & teacher psychoeducation session
Book a screening Ask about this service
At what ages do you screen children?

From infancy through early school years (roughly 18 months to age 10). Assessments follow the child's milestones, so a 3-year-old and a 9-year-old each get a developmentally appropriate battery.

Who pays for a screening?

We work with schools, with most school screening billed as a school program. Individual family screenings are quoted per child before any assessment begins — families should never be asked for payment for a first consultation.

What happens with the results?

Caregivers receive a plain-language report within five working days, with recommended next steps. Any concerns beyond the school's scope are referred with records shared only with the family's consent.

Service 02

Pediatric Applied Behavior Analysis Services (PABA): functional assessments and data-based behavior plans

Pediatric Applied Behavior Analysis Services (PABA) works best when it is taught, practiced and measured across people. Our coaches turn functional behavior assessments into classroom-ready behavior plans, then stay with schools through implementation — so support is consistent between school, home and therapy.

  • Functional behavior assessment (FBA)
  • Positive behavior support planning
  • Teacher & bus-conductor coaching
  • Data-based progress monitoring
Book an assessment Meet the team
Is Pediatric Applied Behavior Analysis Services (PABA) appropriate for my child in school?

When a child's behavior affects learning or participation, a positive, function-based plan can help. An FBA identifies what the behavior communicates, and the plan teaches useful replacement skills with appropriate reinforcement. This can help reduce referrals, early pick-ups, and classroom removals.

How fast can a behavior plan be in place?

Simple plans are typically written within two weeks of intake. Complex cases take longer depending on data collection. You always receive a realistic timeline in writing before work begins.

Do you work with QABA-certified staff?

Yes. Our in-house and partner practitioners include QABA and IBAO certified professionals, and we train many of our own coaches. Contact our team to discuss qualifications and support options for your school or family.

Service 03

School Health Screening: vision, hearing, motor, and wellbeing programs

We bring the screening program to your school: teams set up on-site, children are assessed in batches, and staff and parents receive actionable reports that their boards, clinics, and teachers can act on immediately.

  • Vision & hearing screening clinics
  • Motor & learning-readiness checks
  • Wellbeing & behavior flagging
  • Board-ready outcome reports
Quote a school program Ask about this service
How long does a whole-school program take?

A typical full-school screening covers a school of 300–600 children in one to three weeks of on-site sessions, scheduled around school hours. Reports reach your office within five working days of the final session.

Do screenings require parent consent?

Always. We issue consent forms for school-wide programs in your school's language, and no child is tested without a signed, dated consent. This supports ethical practice and aligns with Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023.

What does the report look like?

You receive a school-level summary for boards and administration, plus individual child profiles for flagged pupils sent to the parent/guardian — never to the whole school. You can request a short briefing call with the lead assessor.

How are group school trainings scheduled?

On-site cohorts run in blocks of 12–20 participants over two to four weeks at your school. Cohorts fill every term; contact us with your head count for a per-seat quote and available block dates.

Do certificates carry recognized value?

Courses follow established competency frameworks and include practical, assessment-based completion rather than attendance certificates alone. Learners receive registration documentation and can pursue external registration where applicable.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes. We reserve scholarship seats for public-school staff and committed school teams, and we price multi-seat school blocks lower per head. Mention your school and head count when you request a syllabus or quote.

About

About Nobelova Gradani

Nobelova Gradani began with educational psychologist Helen Obiageli Oshikoya asking better questions about children's development and learning. Today, we partner with 64 schools across Lagos and have assessed more than 8,000 children through structured developmental screening, Pediatric Applied Behavior Analysis Services (PABA) support, and whole-school health programs.

We believe trust moves faster when roles and qualifications are clear. Our team works with parents, school leaders and educators to explain the support available and coordinate the right next steps.

  • MSc (Educational Psychology)
  • Advanced training in Pediatric Applied Behavior Analysis Services (PABA) (QABA & IBAO-aligned)
  • 12+ years of clinical and school-based practice
  • 64 school partners across Lagos State

Leadership

Helen Obiageli Oshikoya, PhD (Hon.), LLB, BL, MA (Social Sciences)

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Helen Obiageli Oshikoya is an award-winning child advocate, lawyer, and multidisciplinary leader dedicated to helping children be better understood, families make informed decisions, schools respond effectively to diverse needs, and professionals develop the confidence and competence to make a meaningful difference. Her work brings together law, mental healthcare, education, child development, psychoeducational assessment, behavioural practice, and professional training. This interdisciplinary perspective supports a whole-child approach—looking beyond academic performance or behaviour to understand the developmental, emotional, educational, family, and environmental factors that shape a child's experience.

Founder of Nobelova Gradani

In 2012, Helen founded Nobelova Gradani Psychoeducational Services, followed by Nobelova Gradani Training Academy, the dedicated platform for professional education, training, and continuing development.

For parents and families, her work promotes greater understanding and informed decision-making. For schools and institutions, it supports more thoughtful responses to learning, developmental, behavioural, and wellbeing needs. For professionals, the Academy provides opportunities to strengthen knowledge, practical skills, and professional practice.

Her Perspective

Helen's qualifications include LLB, BL, MA, and PhD (Hon.), complemented by specialist credentials in behaviour analysis, autism, educational assessment, and psychological testing.

She believes that meaningful progress begins with understanding the individual, recognising their strengths and needs, and creating the right conditions for development and participation.

As Founder and CEO, Helen remains closely involved in key partnerships, professional appointments, training programmes, and strategic initiatives. Her work is guided by child-centred practice, accountability, collaboration, and meaningful impact.

Through her leadership and advocacy, Helen continues to champion a more informed, inclusive, ethical, and responsive approach to child development, education, mental health, and professional practice across Nigeria.
