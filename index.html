Service 01

Child development screening & psychoeducation

Most learning difficulties are developmental before they are academic. Our play-based assessments look at how a child moves, speaks, plays, attends and regulates — then translate the results into practical guidance for school and home.

  • Gross & fine motor assessment
  • Language & early literacy checks
  • Social & emotional screening
  • Parent & teacher psychoeducation session
Book a screening Ask about this service
At what ages do you screen children?

From infancy through early school years (roughly 18 months to age 10). Assessments follow the child's milestones, so a 3-year-old and a 9-year-old each get a developmentally appropriate battery.

Who pays for a screening?

We work with schools, with most school screening billed as a school program. Individual family screenings are quoted per child before any assessment begins — families should never be asked for payment for a first consultation.

What happens with the results?

Caregivers receive a plain-language report within five working days, with recommended next steps. Any concerns beyond the school's scope are referred with records shared only with the family's consent.

Service 02

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA)

ABA works best when it is taught, practiced and measured across people. Our coaches turn functional behavior assessments into classroom-ready behavior plans, then stay with schools through implementation — so support is consistent between school, home and therapy.

  • Functional behavior assessment (FBA)
  • Positive behavior support planning
  • Teacher & bus-conductor coaching
  • Data-based progress monitoring
Book an assessment Find a QABA practitioner
Is ABA appropriate for my child in school?

For most children with challenging behavior, a positive, function-based program helps — including post-school referral spikes, pick-ups, and room removals in school. An FBA identifies what the behavior is doing for the child, and the plan replaces it with skills and reinforcement.

How fast can a behavior plan be in place?

Simple plans are typically written within two weeks of intake. Complex cases take longer depending on data collection. You always receive a realistic timeline in writing before work begins.

Do you work with QABA-certified staff?

Yes. Our in-house and partner practitioners include QABA and IBAO certified professionals, and we train many of our own coaches. Every practitioner is listed in our verified registry — search any name to confirm credentials and consent.

Service 03

School health screening programs

We bring the screening program to your school: teams set up on-site, children are assessed in batches, and staff and parents receive actionable reports their boards, clinics and teachers can act on immediately.

  • Vision & hearing screening clinics
  • Motor & learning-readiness checks
  • Wellbeing & behavior flagging
  • Board-ready outcome reports
Quote a school program Ask card by card
How long does a whole-school program take?

A typical full-school screening covers a school of 300–600 children in one to three weeks of on-site sessions, scheduled around school hours. Reports reach your office within five working days of the final session.

Do screenings require parent consent?

Always. We issue consent forms for school-wide programs in your school's language, and no child is tested without a signed, dated consent. This aligns with Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 and defensible school ethics.

What does the report look like?

You receive a school-level summary for boards and administration, plus individual child profiles for flagged pupils sent to the parent/guardian — never to the whole school. You can request a short briefing call with the lead assessor.

IBCCES, QABA & IBAO–aligned training

Staff capability is the biggest predictor of child outcomes. We run certification programs on-site in Lagos and virtually, with assessments and national-track preparation built in — so your team leaves with documented, recognized credentials.

  • Levels 1–3 and trainer/coach tracks
  • On-site (Lagos) & virtual cohorts
  • Assessment-based, credentialed completion
  • Group schools & scholarships priced
Enroll staff View course list
How are group school trainings scheduled?

On-site cohorts run in blocks of 12–20 participants over two to four weeks at your school. Cohorts fill every term; contact us with your head count for a per-seat quote and available block dates.

Do certificates carry recognized value?

Courses are built to the published competency structures of IBCCES, QABA, IBAO and BANA, with practical, assessment-based completion rather than attendance certificates alone. Learners receive recognized registration documentation and can pursue external registration where applicable.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes. We reserve scholarship seats for public-school staff and committed school teams, and we price multi-seat school blocks lower per head. Mention your school and head count when you request a syllabus or quote.

About

Founded in 2012 to see what school screening could change.

Nobelova Gradani began as a single educational psychologist reading a child's room and asking better questions. Thirteen years later, our psychoeducation program has partnered with 64 schools across Lagos and assessed more than 8,000 children through structured developmental screening, ABA support and whole-school health programs.

We believe trust moves faster when it is verifiable. That is why every practitioner we place is trained to recognized competency structures and published in a consented, searchable registry — so parents, heads of school and boards can check credentials in seconds.
